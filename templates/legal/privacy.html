Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how News Video Generator ("we", "our", or "the Service") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • OAuth Tokens: When you connect your YouTube or TikTok account, we store authentication tokens to enable video uploads on your behalf.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the Service, such as articles selected and videos generated.
  • Generated Content: Videos, audio files, and images created through the Service are stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service
  • Enable video uploads to your connected social media accounts
  • Improve and optimize the Service

4. Data Storage

All data, including generated videos and authentication tokens, is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your data on external servers, except when interacting with third-party APIs (Azure OpenAI, Google Cloud, YouTube, TikTok) as necessary to provide the Service.

5. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Azure OpenAI: For generating scripts and content
  • Google Cloud: For text-to-speech and image generation
  • YouTube API: For uploading videos to YouTube
  • TikTok API: For uploading videos to TikTok

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their policies.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Authentication tokens are stored locally in JSON files on your device.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Disconnect your social media accounts at any time
  • Delete locally stored data by removing the application files
  • Revoke access through the respective platform's settings (YouTube, TikTok)

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
